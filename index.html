Saturday Receipt

Show me what I actually saved this week, in dollars, on things I already buy. Groceries and fuel. No club. No token. No bank login. Data stays on this phone.

Pilot week: last complete Saturday–Friday. A dollar only counts with a purchase, a paid price, a dated better number, and a source. Demo lines are labelled DEMO. They are not your money.

Household

WA fuel better-price: look up the fill date on FuelWatch, then type the cheaper station you would actually have used.

Grocery lines

One row per item or docket saving. Captured = it was on your docket. Missed = same item, same week, a store you already use was cheaper, sourced.

Fuel fills

Litres × cents per litre you paid. Missed dollars = litres × (paid minus FuelWatch better price within radius).

Plain text (copy or print)

What a dollar is allowed to mean

  1. A purchase you made.
  2. A price you paid.
  3. A documented better number that existed that day.
  4. A source and a date.

Two columns. Never mixed.

Captured — money that did not leave because a special or cheaper pump was used.

Missed — money that left because a sourced cheaper option sat unused.

Unknown — no source. Does not enter either total.

Refused

  • Club discounts you did not use
  • Points that are not cash
  • Tokens, draws, badges, member counts
  • Bank passwords and “review your bills” onboarding
  • A second job

Fee rule (not asked in pilot)

Ask only after two Saturday receipts exist. If captured dollars over four weeks do not beat four weeks of fee, the ask stays off.

Launch pack

This page is the pilot. Eight households. Groceries + fuel. Four weeks. No payment.

  1. Open this page on the phone that goes to the shop.
  2. Each shop: add grocery lines from the docket. Specials on the docket = captured.
  3. Each fill: add litres, cpl paid, station. Check FuelWatch the same day for a cheaper station inside 5 km. If yes, enter it as missed.
  4. Saturday morning: tap Build Saturday receipt.
  5. Send the plain-text receipt to Shawn / Ara if you want a second pair of eyes. You do not have to.
  6. After week 2, say whether the Saturday number changed a decision.

Privacy: everything is stored in this browser only (localStorage). Clearing the site data deletes the pilot. There is no server behind this page.
